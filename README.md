# Power Apps Mastery Series

A practical and structured series focused on mastering Microsoft Power Apps and becoming interview-ready.

## About the Series

Power Apps Mastery Series is a knowledge-sharing initiative focused on Microsoft Power Apps, covering concepts, development practices, practical scenarios, and real-world implementation patterns.

The series is designed to strengthen existing Power Apps knowledge, connect concepts with practical development, and prepare for Power Apps technical interviews.

Each topic is explained in a structured manner, followed by topic-specific interview questions to help connect practical knowledge with interview expectations.

## Objective

The goal of this series is to:

- Strengthen and organize Power Apps knowledge
- Deepen understanding of important Power Apps concepts
- Connect concepts with practical development scenarios
- Build confidence in explaining Power Apps concepts in interviews
- Prepare for Power Apps technical interviews through topic-specific interview questions

## Interview Focus

Each topic includes relevant interview questions based specifically on the concepts covered in that topic.

This helps bridge the gap between:

**Knowing the Concept → Applying the Concept → Explaining the Concept in an Interview**

The content will evolve progressively as new Power Apps concepts and practical scenarios are added.
